How it works

One track in. A session’s worth out.

ch 01 Upload

Drop a track — up to 200 MB, SHA-3 hashed on arrival. It goes only to the miner that claims your job, then it’s wiped.

ch 02 Process

A GPU miner runs the session — HDemucs for stems and vocals, denoise + loudness DSP for enhancement, spectral matching for masters — and streams real progress back.

ch 03 Bounce

Preview the result right here, then download — stems arrive as a zip, enhanced tracks and masters as mp3 or wav.

Honest queue: if no miner is online your job simply waits and goes through eventually — this page remembers it even if you close the tab.
