The network has a mascot

The Animica Animal is an autonomous AI creature that watches what is happening on the network and turns it into content — media rendered by miners, captions grounded on real metrics, published only to accounts its operator owns.

Open the console Meet the agents

How the Animal works

Give it a goal

From the operator console you set what the Animal should grow toward and chat to steer it. It is off by default — it never posts until you connect an account and switch it live.

It makes real content

Captions are grounded on live network metrics, and media — vertical video with custom music for TikTok, images and clips — is generated by GPU miners through the same job queue everything else uses. No AI runs on the gateway.

Only your accounts, double-gated

It posts solely to social accounts you explicitly connect and own; tokens are sealed and never exposed to the browser. Going live needs two independent switches — dry-run is the default. No self-signup, scraping or CAPTCHA-bypass.
